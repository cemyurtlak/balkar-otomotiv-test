How we value your trade-in

We start from the lowest comparable current listing for your car and deduct a margin of 5% to 10% depending on its condition. We show you the calculation openly.

For a more accurate offer

  • Take photos from all four corners, the interior and the dashboard.
  • Mention any service and maintenance records you have.
  • You can check the insurance-claim record via e-Devlet or by SMS to 5664.
